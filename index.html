How the Program Works
Three integrated phases turn students into certified technicians
Phase 1
Lockstep Instruction
Mon - Thurs, 3:00 - 5:00 PM
Daily instruction, guided lab work, and peer accountability. The entire 30-student team moves through the curriculum together. Nobody drifts ahead and nobody gets left behind.
Phase 2
The UPrep Community
Tech Clinic
Students run a real public-facing repair clinic on campus. Real clients. Real devices. Real work orders. That is the experience employers ask about in interviews.
Phase 3
Intensive Testing
June & August
Two dedicated exam weeks for proctored certification exams. Students who pass in June are done. Students who need a retake use August. Everyone walks out certified.

Who Should Apply
We do not select by GPA. We select by potential.

We Are Looking For
- Current UPrep high school student
- Curious about technology
- Willing to commit Mon - Thurs 3 - 5 PM
- Can attend June and August exam weeks
- Any grade level, any GPA
- No prior tech experience needed
❌
This Program Is Not Right If
- You are not a current UPrep student
- You cannot commit to the schedule
- You will miss the June or August exam weeks
