📋 Program Details

  • Open to UPrep high school students — 11th and 12th grade welcome
  • 30 seats only — limited enrollment, application required
  • Mon–Thurs, 3:00–5:00 PM throughout the school year
  • Two summer exam boot camps (June & August)
  • No prior tech experience needed — we start from zero
  • Students selected by logic & grit — not GPA
  • 100% grant-funded — completely free for every student selected
